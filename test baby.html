"test baby"

2026

mixed media animation

inspired by a thoroughly snatched baby doll, these co-created moving images inspect the amount of youth injected into womanhood. we know reclaiming girlhood and whimsy can be healing-- glitter, jewels, and ribbon *are* in fact a reason to get out of bed. but, when hit by the male gaze, these manic pixie signifiers walk a close line with disturbing and unattainable societal expectations.

co-artists: klara kjær, nana blixt vukadinovic, and tilia nørgaard
