"the mural"

2025

acrylic paint, outdoor wall, co-design workshops

depicts a collective vision for nature in mabopane, south africa, co-designed over multiple workshops with local residents, secondary school learners, and university researchers. emergent features include indigenous plants and animals, connection to ancestors, clean water, community action, education, and safe access to nature.

co-artists: brenda ramadiehe, lebo phala, and masego sesana

ps. “leamogetswe” is tswana for “welcome”
