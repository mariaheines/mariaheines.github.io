"qué dolor"

2023

analog photography

the phrase 'qué dolor' has been with me ever since high school spanish class. these two words would leave señor gomez’s mouth when a student-athlete limped in with a fresh cast or after sharing a tragic song about political unrest and heartbreak in his home country of columbia. the repetition of this phrase, alongside these moments, taught me that it was okay to recognize pain. a sensitivity and attention to detail i hope to bring to my own work.
