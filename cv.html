maria heines is a trans-disicplinary artist (b. 1997) with a keen interest in the generative power of art
(i.e. how art is linked to both personal and social-ecological transformation). her practice is
marked by a strong knack for community building, admiration of all that is lively,
and a commitment to critical meaning making in thought and form.
